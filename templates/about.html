Our Story

Who is Kadam
Foundation?

A passionate movement of young changemakers working to uplift communities across India.

Our Foundation

Built on purpose,
driven by people

Kadam Foundation was established with a simple but powerful belief — that young people, when given direction and opportunity, can change the nation. "Kadam" means step in Hindi, and every initiative we run is one step closer to an equal, compassionate India.

We are headquartered in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, and are active across 11 cities, running programs in education, women empowerment, youth welfare, environment protection, and animal welfare.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through education, skill development, and awareness — building a more equal and compassionate India, one step at a time.

Our Vision

A nation where every child has access to education, every woman is empowered, the environment is protected, and no living being is left behind.
